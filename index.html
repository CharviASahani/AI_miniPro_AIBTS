About AIBTS

What happens behind the state?

Every action an AI takes is the result of decisions, calculations and algorithms. AIBTS turns those hidden processes into interactive learning experiences.

Instead of beginning with formulas and definitions, learners begin with something familiar. They play, observe, investigate and then uncover the algorithm underneath.

The goal is simple: make AI something you can experience, not just something you read about.
